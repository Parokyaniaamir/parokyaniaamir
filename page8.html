1. Biology

Biology is the study of living things, including humans, animals, plants, and microorganisms.

2. Physics

Physics studies matter, energy, force, motion, and how the universe works.

3. Chemistry

Chemistry studies substances, their properties, their composition, and how they react with one another.

4. Astronomy

Astronomy studies objects in space, such as stars, planets, moons, galaxies, and the universe.

5. Geology

Geology studies the Earth, including rocks, minerals, Earth's structure, and its history.

6. Ecology

Ecology studies how living organisms interact with one another and with their environment.
